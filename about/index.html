Vladimir Vishar.

Realism tattoo artist · London enquiries now open

I'm Vladimir, a realism tattoo artist accepting enquiries for future London appointments. My practice focuses on two primary disciplines: vivid Colour Realism and evocative Black & Grey work, from detailed portrait tattoos to large-scale sleeve projects.

I also specialise in cover-up tattoos — transforming old or unwanted ink into pieces my clients are genuinely proud of.

Every design I create is a unique collaboration — built from scratch around the client's concept, their body, and their skin. I don't use templates or repeat designs. Each piece is one of a kind.

I use professional single-use equipment wherever required and work to UK tattoo hygiene standards. If you have allergies, medical conditions, or concerns, tell me before booking so the setup can be planned safely.

Project scale

How long larger projects can take

Every project is estimated after reviewing the idea, placement, size, skin, references, and old ink where relevant.

01

Forearm / smaller area

Usually 1-2 full-day sessions, depending on detail and colour.

02

Shoulder / upper arm

Usually 2-3 full-day sessions.

03

Full arm sleeve

Usually 6-8+ sessions for black and grey realism, or 8-10+ sessions for colour realism.

04

Full leg sleeve

Usually around +2 sessions compared with an arm, depending on scale, placement, and complexity.

05

Back / torso / large multi-area projects

Often 6-12+ sessions, depending on scale and complexity.

06

Cover-ups

Always need assessment first. Old ink, darkness, scarring, and available space can change the plan. Cover-ups often take a similar amount of time to colour realism, and some areas may need a refresh after full healing.
