# Manual session receipts

An authorised operator can record the exact amount already received against a tattoo appointment, either as a deposit or a session payment. The CRM records receipt independently of Monzo, including cash and money accepted by the studio. The same operation is available in the CRM and in the plugin. A receipt is one immutable ledger credit. An unresolved older deposit can make the balance unknown without blocking the new receipt. No automatic reconciliation of historic transfers is authorised by this feature.
