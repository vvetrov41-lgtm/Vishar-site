01 · What this book is

Criteria instead of taste

“I like it” and “something feels off” are not professional tools. This manual replaces them with a working system: how the eye enters a design, how light builds volume, how a five-tone map predicts readability, and how the body’s own forms decide where a composition can live. Every chapter ends in criteria you can check on a real project — before the machine is switched on.

It is

  • — A working manual: principles, checklists, diagrams
  • — Written by a working realism artist, from the bench
  • — Objective checks for focus, tone, edges and placement
  • — Built for daily use, not a single read

It is not

  • — Not a portfolio monograph
  • — Not a beginner’s “how to tattoo” course
  • — Not style imitation or recipe copying
  • — Not theory for theory’s sake

02 · Who it’s for

Written for working artists

REALISM ARTISTS

Who want a repeatable way to check focus, tonal structure and edges in a design — instead of sensing that “something is off” after it's healed.

BLACK & GREY AND COLOUR

Both registers share one foundation: tonal contrast outranks colour. The manual works from that base for black & grey and colour realism alike.

LARGE-SCALE PROJECTS

Sleeves, difficult zones, flow across muscle — where composition on paper and composition on the body stop being the same thing.

How the pages will work

These panels are rendered live from the book’s frozen design system — not finished spreads. Final layout and 22 commissioned figures are in production.

Key Principle

If the silhouette and the focal point read in a five-tone map, they will read in the finished work. If everything on the map is the same grey, no amount of detail will save it.

Skin compresses the scale: the working range of a tattoo is narrower than the range of a screen.

At the Skin · Caution

A reference shot in flat, overcast light looks “fine” on screen — and heals into a flat grey mass without form. No application technique compensates for a missing light structure in the design.

07 · Questions

FAQ

When will the book be released?

When it’s ready. The text is complete; the 22 commissioned figures and the final layout are in production, and the release won’t be rushed past them. The waitlist hears first.

What format will it be?

Designed print-first at 170 × 240 mm — a bench-side working format. Digital options will be confirmed closer to release.

What level is it written for?

For working tattoo artists. It assumes you can already tattoo and want a stronger design system — it is not a beginner's introduction to equipment or technique.

Is it only for realism?

It’s written from realism — black & grey and colour — but the core system (focus, tonal mapping, edges, placement, longevity) applies to any style that has to read on a body.

Will there be a sample chapter?

Yes — once the layout is far enough along to show the manual as it will actually look. It will go to the waitlist first.
