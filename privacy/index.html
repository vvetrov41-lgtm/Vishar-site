Privacy & Cookies.

How your data is handled on this site

Who I am

I'm Vladimir Vishar, a self-employed tattoo artist accepting enquiries for future London appointments. This site, vishartattoo.com, is my portfolio and enquiry point. You can contact me directly at info@vishartattoo.com.

What I collect, and why

When you use an enquiry form on this site, you may be asked for your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, Instagram username, preferred reply method, location, tattoo idea, placement, size, preferred timing, cover-up details and reference images. The book waitlist asks only for a name and email address.

Alongside what you type, a tattoo enquiry also records the page you sent it from, the page that referred you, and any campaign tags in the link you followed. That is used to understand which pages bring genuine enquiries, and to recognise a repeated submission so the same enquiry isn't created twice.

This information is used to respond to you and to handle the tattoo request. It is not sold and is not used for unrelated marketing. Sending a form does not reserve an appointment. If the project proceeds, I will explain the deposit, confirmed studio, dates and booking terms before any payment.

How a tattoo enquiry is handled

A tattoo enquiry is stored, not just forwarded. Here is the whole path it takes:

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and runs the private backend that receives the form. It checks the submission and passes it on. It does not keep a copy of your enquiry.
  • Supabase holds the record of the enquiry in a database: your contact details, the project details you described, and the status of the enquiry as I work through it. This is the authoritative record.
  • Supabase Storage holds your reference images in a private area. The images are never publicly accessible and have no public link. They are opened through short-lived, individually issued links that expire within a minute.
  • Telegram receives a short notification telling me a new enquiry has arrived. That notification contains only a reference number and how many images you attached — not your name, contact details or idea.

Every change to an enquiry is written to an internal activity record so there is an accurate history of how a request was handled. That record deliberately holds references and timestamps rather than a second copy of your personal details.

Who can see your enquiry

Access is limited to me and to anyone I explicitly authorise to help with bookings. Each person has a defined level of access, enforced by the database rather than by the interface: someone helping with bookings can read and progress enquiries but cannot see financial figures, manage accounts or change settings, and a read-only account cannot change anything at all. Removing someone's access takes effect immediately.

Nobody can browse your reference images without an account that is permitted to view them, and no member of staff can delete the history of what was done with your enquiry.

Email and calendar

Gmail and Google Calendar are not connected. No enquiry data is sent to either, and no calendar entry is created from this site. If that changes, this notice will be updated before it happens.

When email is enabled in future, only a small set of clearly defined confirmation messages would ever be sent automatically. Anything personal — an estimate, a quote, offered dates, a decline, or a cover-up discussion — would be written or reviewed by a person before it was sent.

Cookies & analytics

No cookies are set when you land on this site. A small banner offers the option to accept optional analytics cookies (Google Analytics) that help me see which pages are useful — they only load if you press Accept. If you press Decline, or close the banner without choosing, nothing is set.

Your cookie choice itself is stored in your browser's localStorage, not a cookie, so the banner doesn't keep reappearing.

Third parties

This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which provides security and delivery for every visit and runs the private backend that receives enquiries. Supabase provides the database and the private file storage that hold tattoo enquiries and reference images. Telegram delivers the short arrival notification described above. Site fonts are self-hosted. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics is used to see aggregate page-visit patterns — no analytics runs before that consent.

Each of these providers processes data on my instructions, for the purpose of running the enquiry and booking process. None of them is given your data for their own marketing.

How long I keep things

Enquiries and reference images are kept for as long as needed to reply and, if you go ahead, to arrange and complete the work — and afterwards where I need the record, for example to discuss a touch-up or to meet a legal obligation.

I have not yet set a fixed retention period, and nothing is deleted automatically. Rather than publish a number I am not actually applying, I will state the period here once it is decided and in force.

If you'd like your enquiry data removed sooner, email me and I'll remove it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, or delete the personal data I hold about you, and to receive a copy of what you gave me. To do so, email info@vishartattoo.com. Please include the enquiry reference number shown when you sent the form if you have it — it makes finding your record quick and avoids me asking you for more details to identify you.

A deletion request covers the enquiry record and the reference images together. If you're not satisfied with how a request is handled, you can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
