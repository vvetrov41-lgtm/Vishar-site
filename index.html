What happens after you send the form

Step 1

You send your idea

The studio form goes to No Regrets. Add your concept, placement, size, references, and whether it is a cover-up.

Step 2

A tattoo consultant gets back to you

The studio team reviews the enquiry and usually replies within two working days. They may ask for more details before booking a consultation.

Step 3

We meet for a consultation

Your consultation can be in person, by video, or by phone. We discuss the idea, references, placement, time estimate, and what is realistic for the tattoo.

Step 4

You choose whether to book

There is no pressure to book during the consultation. After your concept has been reviewed and you decide to book, No Regrets Studios requires a non-refundable chair rental fee to secure the appointment. This comes off the overall tattoo cost.
