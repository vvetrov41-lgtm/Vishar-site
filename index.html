What happens after you send the form

Step 1

You send your idea

The personal form goes directly to me. Add your concept, placement, size, references, and whether it is a cover-up.

Step 2

I review the project

I review the idea, placement, size and references personally. I may ask for more details before arranging a consultation.

Step 3

We meet for a consultation

Your consultation can be in person, by video, or by phone. We discuss the idea, references, placement, time estimate, and what is realistic for the tattoo.

Step 4

You choose whether to book

There is no pressure to book during the consultation. If the project is a good fit, I’ll explain the deposit, available dates, studio details and next steps before you decide.

Approach

How I approach a project

Every piece starts with placement, skin, references, and what the tattoo needs to do on the body. The goal is a custom design that reads clearly now and still holds up over time.

01

Fit first

The design is built around placement, flow, scale, and how the image will sit on the body.

02

Reference-led

03

Realistic scope
