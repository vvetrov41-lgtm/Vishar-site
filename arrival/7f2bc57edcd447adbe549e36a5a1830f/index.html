Finding the studio

Please follow these steps when arriving for your appointment. Everyone must sign in and sign out at reception. Please come alone, as accompanying visitors are not permitted in the studio.

Exhibition House, 16 Addison Bridge Place
London W14 8XP · Third floor

Open Google MapsTattoo preparation FAQ

At the main gate

Find the gate marked Exhibition House on Addison Bridge Place. Press 16 and the bell on the gate intercom, then wait for the gate to open.

Once inside the gates

1

Check in at reception

Walk to Reception at the far end of the driveway, on your right. Sign the visitors' book with your name, reason for visiting, arrival time and signature.

2

Return to the studio entrance

Go back along the driveway to the far-left entrance door shown in the photo. Press 16 and the bell on the building intercom. I'll let you in.

3

Take the lift to the third floor

Once inside, take the lift to floor 3. The studio is on that floor.

Before you leave: Please return to reception and record your departure time in the visitors' book.

If the intercom does not work or you cannot find the entrance, please message me directly.
