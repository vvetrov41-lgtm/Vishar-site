Finding the studio.

Everything you need for your visit, from the entrance gates to the studio on the third floor.

16 Exhibition House, Addison Bridge Place, London W14 8XP

01 / YOUR ARRIVAL

The main gate.

Find the gate marked Exhibition House on Addison Bridge Place. Press 16 and the bell on the intercom, then wait for the gate to open.

02 / ON THE PROPERTY

Reception first. Studio next.

Follow the arrows on the photo. Please visit reception before entering the studio building.

  1. 01

    Check in at reception

    Walk to Reception at the far end of the driveway, on your right. Please sign the visitors' book with your name, reason for visiting, arrival time and signature.

  2. 02

    Enter the studio building

    Go back along the driveway to the far-left entrance door shown in the photo. Press 16 and the bell on the intercom. I'll let you in.

  3. 03

    Third floor

    Take the lift to the third floor. That's where you'll find the studio.

Before you leave: Please return to reception and record your departure time in the visitors' book.

Coming to your appointment: Please come alone. The studio does not allow accompanying guests.

If the intercom does not work or you cannot find the entrance, message me directly.
